# Onboarding recovery release assurance

Candidate versions: plugin 0.5.2 and server 0.3.30. Source base: `73ab9e2bf4779cb996c131e75e614f91200f7e1e`; the delivery record must identify the final reviewed commit. Architecture revision: 22.

The shared client now saves original enrollment identity, baseline and consent before registration, recovers consumed enrollment receipts after response loss, admits existing apply recovery before any fresh apply, settles pending acknowledgements first, and retains completed immutable downloads through canonical advancement. Before checkpoint or apply evidence is retired, the client durably records a catch-up obligation and the ownership of any intermediate local tree; restart resumes that obligation independently of events or status labels, ordinary capture never uploads intermediate rollback ancestry, and a preserved user edit blocks with copy-out/restore guidance instead of queueing a proposal that could never be uploaded. The packaged setup modal resumes from durable state, keeps a missing credential fail-closed, offers explicit same-enrollment consent review when local content changed, and serializes manual and foreground retries. Completed merge completion replay stays idempotent after activation, recovery validation reasons are bounded and specific, and diagnostic JSON reads are capped at 512 KiB with optional sanitized observations for larger journals.

Affected contracts: OBTS-SYNC-ONB-001, OBTS-SYNC-ACK-001, OBTS-SAF-002, OBTS-SAF-005 and OBTS-SAF-009, plus their persistence, credential-isolation and diagnostic obligations. GET connection status adds the original selection, approval baseline and accepted mode for authenticated consumed connections. Completion replay rejects changed identity/mode/baseline and revoked credentials. Deploy the backend before publishing the plugin; old client request and diagnostic payloads remain accepted. Component allocation and trust boundaries are unchanged, so C4 is unchanged.

## Limits and review

No physical iPhone was available. Persistent DataAdapter and subprocess tests do not prove real iOS background suspension, storage power-loss behavior or all native Obsidian editor interactions. They exercise application-process recovery and the mobile metadata visibility barrier. Malformed or ambiguous historical state, unavailable original enrollment evidence and missing/corrupt preservation roots remain specifically blocked; no automatic journal deletion, credential reset or semantic winner selection is authorized.

Independent review covered the change in two rounds with fresh-context reviewers: a first round on the original repair (docs/formal/trace consistency, enrollment receipt/replay, checkpoint/apply safety, mobile lifecycle and diagnostics) and a second round on the correction pass (per-item verification plus adversarial catch-up/ancestry falsification). All accepted findings were fixed before the final validation, including the durable catch-up obligation, intermediate-ancestry upload blocking, preserved-edit recovery guidance, cross-pairing journal cleanup, bounded catch-up recursion, missing-credential fail-closed handling, same-enrollment consent review, idempotent completed-merge replay, specific recovery validation reasons and the true mid-apply SIGKILL seam. One known cosmetic residue is not fixed: `.obts/*.json.diagnostic-summary` sidecars are not removed when their journal is removed; they are freshness-checked against size and mtime and are never recovery authority.

Completion of current-tree full Vitest (recorded above), final commit identity, backend delivery and BRAT release verification belong to the landing record. This document does not claim deployment or real-device validation.
